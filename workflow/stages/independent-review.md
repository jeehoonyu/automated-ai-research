# Independent review

Use only the question, claim text, evidence, source metadata, contradictions, and rubric. Exclude primary
rationale, confidence, earlier reviews, suggested wording, and hidden reasoning. Record the complete
independence declaration and a typed support assessment in a `Review` artifact. Do not emit or inspect a
full Claim artifact. After the review passes structural validation, the CLI creates the superseding claim
status version deterministically without exposing primary confidence fields to the reviewer.
