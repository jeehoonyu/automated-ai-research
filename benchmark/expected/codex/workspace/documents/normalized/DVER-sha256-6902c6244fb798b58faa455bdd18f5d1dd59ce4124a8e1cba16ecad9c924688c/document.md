# Synthetic Cache Study

Authors: A. Researcher and B. Analyst

## Method

The synthetic experiment compared a baseline cache with a partitioned cache over 120 generated
workloads. The experiment reused one simulator and did not measure production latency.

## Results

The partitioned cache reduced mean simulated data movement by 18 percent relative to the baseline.
The reported 95 percent confidence interval ranged from 12 percent to 24 percent.

## Limitations

The workloads were synthetic, so the result must not be generalized directly to production systems.
